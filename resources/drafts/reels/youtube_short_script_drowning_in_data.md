**Target Audience:** Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs)

---

    *   **On-Screen Text:** "Is Excel eating your day?"

    *   **On-Screen Text:** "Unlock AI Power!" "Simplify Data Management!" "Gain Clear Insights!"

*   **40-55s (Call-to-Action):** User (now confident, productive) easily navigating the AI-generated report, making quick decisions.

    *   **Audio:** Music swells slightly. Voiceover: "Stop getting lost in data. Start making smarter decisions faster. It's time to empower your SME with intelligent automation."
